# 📱 MS Local Play - Website & Privacy Policy

This repository contains the official, Google Play Store-compliant Landing Page (`index.html`) and Privacy Policy (`privacy.html`) for **MS Local Play** (Developed by Mukesh Saw).

---

## 🌐 Pages Overview:
1. **`index.html`** - Sleek Landing Page with Hero section, feature cards, and Download button.
2. **`privacy.html`** - Google Play Policy compliant Privacy Policy web page.

---

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Instant Free Hosting):

1. **Upload Files:** Upload `index.html`, `privacy.html`, and `README.md` to your GitHub repository.
2. **Open Repository Settings:** On GitHub, go to your repository -> **Settings** tab.
3. **Enable Pages:** On the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
4. **Select Branch:** Under **Source / Branch**, choose `main` (or `master`) and keep folder as `/ (root)`.
5. **Save:** Click **Save**.
6. **Live URLs:**
   - **Landing Page:** `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`
   - **Privacy Policy Page:** `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/privacy.html`
7. **Paste into Play Console:** Copy your live Privacy Policy URL and paste it into **Google Play Console** -> **App content** -> **Privacy policy**!
