MS Local Play • Developed by Mukesh Saw (Dostified)

🛡️ Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to MS Local Play. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles information when you use our local media player and streaming features.

Core Guarantee: MS Local Play is designed as a local-first application. All media indexing, video playback, photo browsing, and music playback operations run 100% on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal files or user data.

🔑 Permissions & Their Exact Usage

To provide local playback and local Wi-Fi casting capabilities, MS Local Play requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Media & Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used exclusively to discover and display your local videos, photos, and music files within the app. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Local Network & Wi-Fi Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES): Used strictly to enable local HTTP and DLNA streaming (`http://192.168.x.x:8080`) over your home Wi-Fi network to your own Jio STB, Smart TV, or web browser.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to keep the local media server active in the background and display system media player controls (Play, Pause, Next, Previous) in your Notification Center.

🚫 Data Collection & Third-Party Sharing

  • No Advertising SDKs: MS Local Play contains no third-party ad networks or tracking libraries.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not track your usage habits, device identifiers, or playback history.
  • No Data Sales: Your data is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

👶 Children's Privacy

MS Local Play is a general audience local media player. The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
